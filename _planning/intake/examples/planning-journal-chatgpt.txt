The V1 thesis

Before Lindsay Clancy became the defendant in a murder trial, she spent months documenting that she was becoming unable to function, repeatedly seeking help, cycling through psychiatric treatment, and continuing to carry responsibility for three young children. Icarus reconstructs that period from her journal, medical record, communications, witness testimony, household activity, physical evidence, and the trial record—without allowing any single person or institution to become the narrator by default.

The prosecution has explicitly urged jurors to focus on Lindsay’s actions rather than turn the trial into a debate about women’s mental healthcare. Your work can show why those cannot be fully separated.

This case contains several distinct questions:

What did Lindsay physically do?
What was her mental and physical state at that time?
What did her family and clinicians know before it happened?
What care did she seek and receive?
What responsibilities remained assigned to her?
How did the criminal narrative form afterward?
What does the case expose about the treatment of postpartum women generally?

The parties reportedly do not dispute that Lindsay caused the children’s deaths. The central legal dispute is criminal responsibility. That gives your reconstruction a sharper purpose: it does not need to manufacture an alternative perpetrator to challenge the completeness of the prevailing story.

It can ask:

What had to happen to this woman—in her body, brain, household, and healthcare system—for January 24 to become possible?

The incident window

A constrained reconstruction using timestamps, calls, locations, physical evidence, medical evidence, and explicit uncertainty.

The narrative capture

How attributed statements became police assumptions, media facts, prosecution and defense theories, and public beliefs.

The postpartum story

What her case reveals about treating maternal distress as a defect inside one woman while leaving her care burden, sleep, relationships, treatment fragmentation, and operating environment largely unmeasured.

The principle that protects the work

The project cannot begin with save Lindsay, convict Lindsay, or prove Patrick did it. It begins with:

Preserve her voice. Reconstruct the conditions. Test every account equally. Show the evidence and uncertainty.

That discipline is what gives the work moral and analytical force. If the reconstruction strengthens a prosecution claim, it stays. If it exposes a contradiction, omitted condition, or unsupported assumption, that stays too.

And I think the heart of it is this:

Lindsay documented herself disappearing in real time. The institutions around her recorded fragments. The court is now judging the final catastrophic interval. Icarus reconstructs the woman, the environment, and the months that existed before it.

That tells her story without speaking over her—and creates a framework for recognizing other postpartum women before their suffering becomes a tragedy, a diagnosis attached only to them, or a criminal case.
