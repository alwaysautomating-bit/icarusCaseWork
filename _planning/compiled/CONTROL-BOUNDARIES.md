# Control Boundaries

## Authority Model

The human researcher controls case scope, high-impact claim acceptance, entity merges, event promotion, contradiction disposition, hypothesis framing, and publication approval. Model-assisted operations propose; they do not silently change authoritative case state.

## Deterministic Operations

- cryptographic hashing and artifact deduplication;
- evidence-snapshot manifests;
- explicit timestamp normalization;
- exact citation addressing;
- declared constraint evaluation;
- lineage traversal;
- export validation against required metadata.

## Model-Assisted Operations

- text and media extraction;
- attributed claim candidate generation;
- entity and alias suggestions;
- contradiction and dependency suggestions;
- uncertainty-aware event candidates;
- narrative drafting from reviewed records.

Every model-assisted output must retain model/version metadata, input references, status, and reviewer disposition.

## Human-Only Decisions in V1

- accepting a high-impact claim;
- promoting a claim-supported observation to a reviewed event;
- merging identities when the merge changes interpretation;
- declaring a contradiction resolved;
- defining a competing account and its required conditions;
- approving publishable output;
- deciding whether sensitive public information is necessary to include.

## Safety Boundaries

- Use only public records, authorized uploads, and creator-supplied lawful research material.
- Do not contact relatives, witnesses, clinicians, jurors, or parties without a separate approved protocol.
- Do not determine guilt, legal insanity, diagnosis, credibility, motive, or admissibility.
- Do not present feasibility frequency as probability that an account is true.
- Protect children and minimize graphic, identifying, medical, and family detail.
- Require crisis-resource language in public material addressing suicide, postpartum psychosis, or harm to children.
- Treat model output as a proposal or projection, never as source evidence.

## Epistemic Invariants

1. A source statement is not a verified event.
2. Repetition from one origin is not independent corroboration.
3. Event time, statement time, publication time, and ingestion time remain distinct.
4. Contradictory evidence may coexist without forced resolution.
5. Absence of evidence remains unknown unless a declared rule supports a stronger inference.
6. Every material narrative assertion has an exact citation or is visibly labeled interpretation.
7. A projection cannot overwrite the evidence substrate.
8. Unresolved consequential conditions remain surfaced until a recorded terminal disposition is evidenced.

## Publication Gate

An export is publishable only when automated validation and an independent human review confirm:

- sentence-level source coverage;
- correct attribution and uncertainty language;
- lineage-aware corroboration counts;
- public-record cutoff date and evidence snapshot;
- no prohibited guilt, diagnosis, credibility, admissibility, or truth-probability claims;
- case-specific safety and dignity review.
