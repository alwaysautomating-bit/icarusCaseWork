# Compiled Decisions

## 08-13-2026 — Evidence substrate is authoritative

Decision: immutable artifacts, attributed claims, reviewed events, entities, relationships, chronology, provenance, and uncertainty form the authoritative substrate.

Reason: analytical and narrative outputs must remain reproducible and cannot redefine their inputs.

## 08-13-2026 — Claim is first-class; hypothesis is a projection

Decision: persist claims as attributed assertions. Treat hypotheses and probabilistic results as projections over an evidence snapshot.

Reason: this prevents statements and analytical accounts from becoming facts by implementation accident.

## 08-13-2026 — True-crime creator is the V1 wedge

Decision: design V1 for true-crime researchers and long-form creators using public or authorized material, demonstrated with the Lindsay Clancy reconstruction.

Reason: it proves provenance, chronology, contradiction, and narrative lineage without claiming readiness for regulated or enforcement workflows.

## 08-13-2026 — Human review governs consequential transitions

Decision: high-impact claim acceptance, event promotion, consequential identity merges, contradiction resolution, scenario framing, and publication approval require recorded human decisions.

Reason: model assistance cannot supply the missing authority or evidence for consequential state changes.

## 08-13-2026 — Build by gated vertical slices

Decision: deliver a thin source-to-timeline slice first, then grow the evidence graph, researcher workflow, narrative export, and constrained Scenario Lab.

Reason: each slice produces observable evidence for the V1 proof obligation and limits premature platform expansion.
