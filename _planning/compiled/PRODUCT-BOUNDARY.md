# Product Boundary

## Current Product Boundary

Icarus Casework is currently defined as a case-reconstruction workspace for heterogeneous, source-linked public-record analysis.

It is not currently defined as:

- a truth engine;
- a verdict engine;
- a diagnosis engine;
- a court-admissibility engine;
- a clinical decision system;
- a law-enforcement operational platform; or
- a generalized monorepo for all future Icarus reasoning products.

## First Wedge

The first wedge is true-crime and adjacent public-record research because it is sufficiently hard to demonstrate the architecture while avoiding immediate regulated-data and enforcement workflows.

This wedge proves whether Icarus can:

- preserve provenance;
- separate claims from evidence;
- reconstruct chronology;
- represent contradictions and unknowns;
- expose narrative capture and claim lineage; and
- produce supportable narrative outputs.

## Core Substrate vs. Domain Layer

Core substrate:

- source artifacts
- source provenance
- entities
- events
- evidence
- relationships
- chronology
- uncertainty

Casework domain layer:

- claims
- witness observations
- filings
- proceedings
- system actors
- competing accounts
- case chronology

Reasoning / analysis layer:

- contradiction analysis
- corroboration independence
- temporal feasibility
- bounded state inference
- care or system fragmentation analysis
- scenario comparison

Projection layer:

- timeline
- evidence map
- source reader
- case brief
- cited narrative builder

Advanced / later modules:

- Scenario Lab as a richer workbench
- Monte Carlo reconstruction
- Bayesian proposition comparison
- responsibility allocation
- pattern-of-life analysis

## Boundary Rules

- Preserve the substrate even when domain framing changes.
- Do not let later probabilistic or scenario-testing modules redefine the authoritative evidence graph.
- Do not allow narrative outputs to collapse uncertainty or attribution.
- Do not absorb advanced modules into V1 just because planning material exists for them.
- Keep doctrine upstream where possible; only snapshot into Casework what the build directly depends on.

## Current Scope Decision

Current V1 should prove the substrate and public-record case reconstruction loop first.

This means V1 should prioritize:

- attributed source ingestion;
- explicit claim/evidence separation;
- layered timeline construction;
- contradiction and gap surfacing;
- source-linked narrative output.

This means V1 should not yet require:

- final probabilistic subsystem commitments beyond constrained feasibility testing;
- full trial transcript ingestion for every case;
- generalized healthcare-grade modeling;
- enterprise governance layers;
- regulated workflow support.

## Approved Boundary Decisions

- `Claim` is a first-class evidentiary assertion.
- `Hypothesis` is a Projection, not a substrate object.
- The first wedge remains explicitly true-crime and public-record research first.
- Probabilistic output is always a Projection.
- V1 may include constrained feasibility testing, but the evidence substrate remains authoritative.

The approved substrate sequence is:

`Immutable artifacts -> Attributed claims -> Reviewed observable events -> Entities + relationships + chronology -> Evidence graph -> Patterns, hypotheses, priors, simulations, and narrative projections`

## Current Compiler Conclusion

The product boundary is coherent enough for kickoff. Future probabilistic and scenario capabilities are bounded behind the substrate rather than defining it.
